ABOUT ME

Notes from
the studio

Yajoo makes music the way static settles into a song — part analog warmth, part restless electricity. Raised on tape hiss and late-night radio, the work moves between quiet, unfinished-sounding demos and full-band noise, never staying in one shape for long.

Every release starts as a voice memo, a loop, or a mistake worth keeping.
